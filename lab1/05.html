Simple Pancake Recipe

Ingredients

  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1 tablespoon sugar
  • 1 teaspoon baking powder
  • 1 egg
  • 3/4 cup milk

Instructions

  1. Mix the flour, sugar, and baking powder in a bowl.
  2. Whisk the egg and milk together, then stir into the dry ingredients.
  3. Cook small portions in a lightly greased pan until golden on both sides.
